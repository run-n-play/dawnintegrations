I took a couple of days away from the screen to work for my wife's business, **The Cows Nest**. She needed a display area, so I built her this table and cut a set of blocks to go with it.

![The finished display table — stained pine top over white painted poplar base with two drawers](/assets/images/display-table/display-table-finished.jpg)

## Materials: salvage and shop-milled

- **Top** — built from the shipping 4x4s that were used to crate machines over from France. Too good to throw out, and the grain shows up nicely under stain.
- **Base and drawers** — poplar, sawed at our cabinet shop's mill.

## Legs: 4th axis, then a taper jig

I cut the legs on the CNC using the **4th axis**. Once they were done, my wife decided she wanted a **tapered leg** — so I built a jig to cut the angles and ran them through it.

## Drawers

The drawers are **solid wood**, milled down to **5/8"** and rounded over with a **1/8" router bit**.

## Finish

Stained top, painted legs and base. I like the contrast of the wood top against the white myself — hope you all do too.
